Problem Statement
The customer experience on SAP.com misses opportunities to create moments of meaningful engagement and delight.
  • Interactions can feel static and impersonal, creating a one-way experience rather than an engaging digital journey.
  • The lack of visual feedback and other dynamic cues can make workflows feel less polished and satisfying.
  • The result is an experience that feels more perfunctory than memorable, and limits our ability to strengthen user connection, confidence, and overall perception of the brand.

Solution Statement
Thoughtful micro-animations act as communication tools that make SAP.com feel more engaging, responsive, and…delightful!
  • Immediate visual and audio feedback acknowledges user actions and helps create a smoother, more seamless experience.
  • Relevant motion cues can guide attention and behavior without adding complexity.
  • Subtle animations reinforce successful interactions and help users navigate through workflows with greater confidence.

Why it Matters
Effective micro-animations don't just add delight, they help create an experience that's:
easier to understand
simpler to use
more likely to be remembered

Animation isn't decoration — it's communication. Every transition, every hover state, every loading indicator is a micro-conversation between the product and the user.

When done well, motion reduces cognitive load, guides attention naturally, and turns functional moments into ones users actually remember.
